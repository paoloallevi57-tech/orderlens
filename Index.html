Accuracy, privacy and limitations

Text extraction uses Tesseract.js locally in your browser, with Greek, English and Italian language models. Searchable PDFs are read directly when possible. Product tables are analysed by position, prioritising descriptions and quantities even when an item code cannot be read. Greek → English matching uses an on-device bilingual terminology dictionary and checks brand, series, colour/shade and pack. It is not a full machine translation model; ambiguous matches must be checked manually. If product rows are missed, use Find & scan products or drag across the product rows. A second OCR pass runs on your selection and always shows the recognized text and editable proposals, even if no row could be detected. The camera scanner can assemble images into a downloadable PDF directly on your device. Customer and product identification use transparent heuristics, not a cloud AI model; layout differences, crumpled photos, handwriting and multi-column invoices may require corrections. The app does not send uploaded files or the product master to an OCR server. Human-approved mappings and selected table regions may be saved in this browser’s local storage only when confirmed. Loading more unlabelled files alone does not train the OCR engine. External libraries and OCR language packs are downloaded from public CDNs, which receive normal network requests. The hosted site is public to anyone with its link; do not upload confidential documents without the appropriate permission.
